How to Play

  • ← / → or D-Pad Left/Right: Move piece horizontally.
  • ↓ or D-Pad Down: Soft drop (move piece down faster).
  • ↑, 'A' Button (Gamepad), or D-Pad Up: Rotate piece.
  • Spacebar or 'Y' Button (Gamepad): Hard drop (instantly drop piece).
  • C or Hold Button: Hold current piece / Swap with held piece.
  • P, Start/Select (Gamepad): Pause/Continue game.
  • M or Touch Music Icon: Mute/Unmute sound.

Clear lines by filling all squares in a row. The game ends when a new tetromino cannot be placed at the top of the grid. Consecutive line clears build combos for more points!
