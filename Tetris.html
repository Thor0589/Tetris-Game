How to Play

  • Left/Right Arrows or D-Pad Left/Right: Move horizontally.
  • Down Arrow or D-Pad Down: Soft drop.
  • Up Arrow, 'A' Button, or D-Pad Up: Rotate.
  • Spacebar or 'Y' Button: Hard drop.
  • P, Start, or Select Button: Pause/Continue.
  • M or Touch Music Icon: Mute/Unmute sound.

Clear lines by filling all squares in a row. The game ends when a new tetromino cannot be placed at the top of the grid.
